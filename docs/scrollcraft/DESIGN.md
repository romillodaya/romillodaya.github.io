# Design and score

## Grammar and fingerprint gate

Chaptered editorial. Natural document flow, no fixed top bar, no photography behind copy, no full-bleed scrub hero, no pinned crossfades, no magnetic buttons. A small desktop margin folio follows the current chapter. Mobile hides the margin folio and uses the standard top navigation, leaving the narrow reading column clear.

Other grammars ruled out: filmic one-shot forces an argument onto a notebook; live surface is for an actual tool; continuous world would hide the archives behind a journey; typographic poster disallows the photographic stories; gallery makes writing subordinate to objects; split stage implies a comparison that does not exist; rhythmic cutlist is too restless for reading.

The local fingerprint registry is empty. Gate passes with zero prior rows; no history has been changed.

## Score, after the feeling curve in BRIEF.md

| Beat | Family | Natural span | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Welcome | flow + in | ~0.6 screen | Stable typographic title page, small entrance on introduction |
| Interest | reveal | ~0.65 screen | A ruled opening reveals the notebook boundary, all writing stays readable |
| Delight (peak) | parallax + page-local bookmark choreography | ~1.05 screen desktop, stacked on phone | Contact sheet, large print and excerpt; independent print/frame/marker motion with a selected story carried forward |
| Curiosity | flow + in | ~0.4 screen | Short project notes with no forced dwell |
| Invitation | kinetic (one final heading) | ~0.35 screen | Quiet text-led colophon; selected story destination remains continuously available |

Four device families: flow/in, reveal, parallax, kinetic. No family repeats adjacently. No scrub, pin, horizontal page hijacking, empty viewport, full-photo text overlay, or counters. The peak is the largest section, but length follows content instead of padding quotas.

## Layer contract

The editorial grammar forbids a layered photographic marketing hero. The title page uses stable semantic typography. Depth belongs only to the photographic chapter.

| Plane | Asset | Movement | Contact/occlusion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ground | Neutral chapter paper | None | No image under text |
| Print | Selected real sample photograph | Restrained entry reveal, small scale settle | Cropped in its own frame; mountain peak retained on phone |
| Frame | Border and caption support | Moves with the print as a group | Caption remains outside the crop |
| Near strip | Three actual photo-selection buttons | Small independent parallax | All tap targets remain visible; no decorative duplicates covering the main photograph |
| Bookmark | Blue selection indicator | Travels between selected thumbnails | Follows real selected state; static on reduced motion |
| Type/controls | Heading, excerpt, source link, reading destination | Stable | Never hidden behind an image |

Opening: selected photo visible as an inset print. Midpoint: print opens to its full frame while the contact strip shifts slightly against it. Exit: print holds at full width, and the selected memory is repeated in the closing reading link. Mobile stacks heading, strip, print and text with a shallower reveal; reduced motion presents the resolved frame.

The next view cuts to a compact book collection and then biography. Adapt the separation of media and copy and the change in section density, not its book sales layout.

At the intermediate position the right column remains a readable compact list alongside larger entries. Adapt mixed reading lengths and asymmetry, not her illustrations, palette or wording.
