## Eight creative topics

1. Vibe: user supplied clean, minimal, modern, fun. Authored reference: a well-typeset literary journal with a photographic contact sheet tucked between pages.
2. Journey: authored under delegation. Meet Romil, browse writing, open a photographic memory, inspect a small project, choose something to read.
3. Energy: authored. Quiet title, readable substance, one photographic expansion, then a quiet resolution.
4. Emotion: authored. Welcome, interest, delight, curiosity, invitation. The travel spread carries the remembered moment.
5. Signature seed: authored. A visitor moves a bookmark between three photographic memories; the selected frame, excerpt, date, and reading destination all follow that choice. The contact sheet unfolds into a large framed photograph as it enters view.
6. Aesthetic range: user explicitly requested clean and minimal. Choose editorial; avoid cinematic pinning and oversized spectacle.
7. World: authored distinct printed chapters, no continuous world or flythrough.
8. Assets: reuse three verified, credited Unsplash sample photographs already in the demo. No owned portrait, brand kit, video, or genuine travel photos supplied. No asset generation or API spending required.

## Feeling curve (written before the score)

- Welcome: a left-aligned title and a short personal introduction on open paper.
- Interest: a large featured essay with a calm, compact list of recent entries.
- Delight: a contact strip opens into a photographic spread; moving the bookmark changes the memory and its associated story.
- Curiosity: a compact workbench, plain descriptions with actual project destinations.
- Invitation: a quiet reading-room colophon carries the selected memory into one final “Read the story” link.
